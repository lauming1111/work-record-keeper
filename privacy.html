Privacy & Storage

Last updated 23 August 2026

Work Record Keeper has no server, no account system and no analytics. Nothing you type into it is sent anywhere. It stays in your browser, on your device, until you delete it.

Why there is no cookie banner

Because the site sets no cookies. Not one — not for analytics, not for advertising, not for sessions.

The app does use your browser's localStorage, which is a different mechanism. Consent rules cover storage that is not needed to deliver what you asked for; the storage described below is the feature. It holds your own hours and settings, on your own device, and is never transmitted. Putting a consent banner in front of that would ask you to approve something that never happens, which makes real banners easier to ignore.

What is stored on your device

WhatWhy
Hours, start and end times, lunch minutes, per dayThe record you are keeping
Hourly rate, start date, pay cycleTo work out pay and deductions
Your job list and which one is activeMulti-job support
Your buy list and its pricesProgress tracking
Roster images you uploadStored as data in your browser; never uploaded anywhere
Dark mode, language, combine-jobs toggleRemembering your preferences

That is the whole list. No name, no email, no address, no employer details, no device identifier, no location.

This also means there is no backup. Clearing your browser data, using private browsing, or switching device or browser loses everything. Use Export Data in the app to keep a copy.

How to delete it

  • Clear All in the app removes that job's data.
  • Clearing site data for this domain in your browser settings removes all of it.

Neither needs to involve us, because there is nothing on our side to delete.

Who else is involved

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives the requests your browser makes to load the page and may log technical details such as your IP address and browser version. That is GitHub's processing under GitHub's privacy statement, outside our control, and it happens for any site hosted there.

Beyond the host, the app loads nothing from any third party — no fonts, no CDN, no scripts, no tracking pixels. It makes no network requests of its own at all while you use it.

The footer links to a personal profile page. Following it is a normal outbound link and only happens if you click it.

Location and automations

The app does not request or read your location. It cannot: it has no geolocation code.

If you set up an arrive-at-work automation using iOS Shortcuts or an Android routine, the location matching happens entirely on your phone, by your operating system. All it does is open a link. Your location is never sent to this site and this site never learns where you are.

Children

The app is a personal calculator with no accounts and no data collection, so it is not directed at children and gathers nothing from anyone regardless of age.

Changes

If this ever changes — if the app gains a backend, an account, or any analytics — this page will be updated before that ships, and the date at the top will move.
