Terms & Disclaimer

Last updated 23 August 2026

Work Record Keeper is a personal estimator. It is not payroll software, not an accounting record, and not tax advice. Do not rely on it to file a return, to check a payslip is correct, or to support a dispute with an employer.

What the tax figures actually are

The app follows the shape of the Canada Revenue Agency's payroll deduction formulas for Ontario, using published rates for each year. It works out withholding — roughly what comes off a paycheque — for federal and Ontario income tax, CPP and EI.

These figures are an estimate, and they are deliberately incomplete.

Credits it leaves out

Refundable credits that are settled when you file, rather than at each paycheque, are not included — the same way they are absent from the payroll formulas themselves. The two that matter most at lower incomes are the Ontario LIFT credit and the Canada Workers Benefit. Because of this, a low earner's real tax for the year will normally be lower than the total this app shows as withheld.

Personal credits beyond the basics are also not modelled: dependants, tuition, disability amounts, spousal amounts, RRSP contributions, and anything else you would claim on a TD1 form. If you claim any of those, less tax comes off your real pay than the app estimates.

Other limits worth knowing

  • Ontario only. The provincial brackets, surtax, tax reduction and health premium are Ontario's. Figures will be wrong for any other province, and Quebec differs substantially, including its own EI rate and pension plan.
  • Annual maximums restart per job. CPP and EI ceilings are tracked separately for each job you enter, which mirrors how each employer deducts. If you hold more than one job, your combined deductions can exceed the annual maximum during the year and be reconciled when you file.
  • A shift crossing midnight is not supported. Hours are worked out from a start and end within one date, so an end time earlier than the start records no hours at all.
  • Rates are held per year and only for the years listed in the app. A date outside those years falls back to the nearest year in the table, which will not be accurate.
  • Your employer's actual payroll may differ in rounding, pay period alignment, benefit deductions, union dues, garnishments and taxable benefits — none of which this app knows about.
If a number here disagrees with your payslip, your payslip is the record. For anything that matters, use the CRA's Payroll Deductions Online Calculator, or speak to your employer's payroll department or a qualified accountant.

The bi-weekly rule option

The app contains an alternative pay rule, enabled by naming a job a particular way, which treats hours past a threshold as untaxed rather than as overtime. It exists to let someone model an arrangement they have actually been given. Including it is not advice that such an arrangement is lawful, and it is not a suggestion to seek one. Employment standards and tax obligations are set by law, not by how anyone chooses to pay.

Your data is your responsibility

Everything is stored in your browser and nowhere else. There is no account, no server copy and no way to recover anything. Clearing browser data, browsing privately, or changing device or browser loses it permanently. Use Export Data regularly if the record matters to you. See the Privacy & Storage page.
